# Frequently Asked Questions

If you cannot find what you need, use the **search** box in the upper-right
corner or visit the [Contact](iletisim.md) page.

## Eligibility & Timing {: #uygunluk-zamanlama }

??? question "Do I find my own internship, or does the department place me?"
    **You find the internship yourself.** The department does not place students
    directly. Internship postings and announcements are sent to students
    registered through the **[Internship Registration Form]({{ baglantilar.kayit_formu }})**.
    The form is only for announcements and the internship database; it does not
    assign you to a company.

??? question "Do I need to register in the system or database before starting?"
    **Yes.** Complete the **[Internship Registration Form]({{ baglantilar.kayit_formu }})**
    to join the internship database and receive announcements. See
    [Before the Internship](surec/index.md#oncesi).

??? question "When can I complete an internship? Can I do one during an academic term?"
    Complete internships during periods when you are **not taking courses**, such
    as summer or semester breaks. The deciding factor is whether you are taking
    courses, not the name of the term. Internship dates cannot overlap with a
    Fall semester, Spring semester, or Summer School in which you are enrolled
    in courses. If you
    are not taking courses because you have completed them or are on an exchange
    program, you may complete an internship during the term. A **special
    submission deadline** then applies; see the questions below and the
    [Rules](kurallar.md).

??? question "After which semester may I begin an internship?"
    Students are generally advised to begin after completing the
    **fourth-semester** courses. Irregular students may also begin after the
    **third semester**. See the [Rules](kurallar.md).

??? question "Can I complete an internship while attending Summer School?"
    The deciding factor is whether you are **taking courses**. Internship dates
    cannot overlap with Summer School courses. If there is a special circumstance,
    such as graduation, first consult the [internship assistants](iletisim.md)
    and then the Internship advisor. If you are not taking courses, you may
    complete the internship normally.

??? question "I will not take courses during the term. Can I complete an internship?"
    **Yes.** If you are taking no courses because you have completed them or are
    on Erasmus or another exchange program, you may complete an internship
    during that term. A **special report deadline** applies: submit the report
    within **three weeks of completing the internship**. See the
    [Rules](kurallar.md).

??? question "I have completed my courses and am close to graduation. Can I complete an internship?"
    You may complete an internship while your **student registration remains
    active** and before you graduate. If you are unsure, consult your academic
    advisor or the internship assistants.

??? question "Can I complete an internship abroad?"
    Yes. Most rules also apply to internships abroad, but Türkiye-specific
    procedures such as the **EK-1, government contribution, and insurance** do
    not. Consult the [Internship advisor or assistants](iletisim.md) first.

??? question "Are part-time or remote internships accepted?"
    **Remote internships are accepted**, and the days count normally. The
    internship must still be **full-time**; part-time internships are not accepted.

??? question "Can my current job or long-term internship count as my compulsory internship?"
    **Yes**, provided that it complies with the [internship rules](kurallar.md).
    You may complete a compulsory internship as part of a longer program.

??? question "I am a transfer student. Do my previous internships count?"
    Workdays from previous internships may count toward your General IE Internship.

??? question "I am a double-major (ÇAP) student. Is my internship-day requirement different?"
    **It depends on your primary major.** If your primary major **is IE**, the
    requirements are the same: 40 workdays, including at least 15 Production
    Internship days. If your primary major **is not IE**, only **at least 15
    workdays of Production Internship** are required on the IE side — the
    40-day total requirement does not apply. See the [Rules](kurallar.md).

??? question "I am a minor (Yandal) student. Do I need to complete an internship for IE?"
    **No.** Minor students have no internship requirement on the IE side.

??? question "Does work at a university or as a research assistant count?"
    **No.** Work completed in a university setting, including research
    assistantships, does not count as an internship. See the
    [Rules](kurallar.md).

## Duration & Workday Counting

??? question q-sure "How many internship days are required for graduation?"
    You must complete **40 workdays** in total. The only type requirement is that
    at least **15 days must be a Production Internship**; the remaining days carry
    no type restriction — they may be a General IE Internship, or further
    Production Internship days.

??? question q-sure "What is the minimum length of one internship?"
    Each internship must last at least **15 workdays** to be accepted.

??? question q-sure "Can I split my internship requirement across companies or periods?"
    **Yes.** You may complete the 40 workdays at different companies or during
    different periods. Each internship must last at least **15 workdays**, and
    the total must include at least **15 Production Internship days**.

??? question q-sure "Do Saturdays and public holidays count as workdays?"
    A **Saturday** counts if you obtain a **signed or stamped Saturday Work
    Letter** from the company and submit it with the report. **Public holidays
    do not count**.

??? question q-sure "Can I take leave during the internship?"
    Leave is decided by the **company**; department approval is not required.
    If the company grants leave, you may take it, but those days are
    **deducted from your internship days**.

??? question q-sure "Can I complete more than 40 days? Is there a maximum?"
    University insurance is intended for the compulsory internship, so keep the
    total **as close to 40 days as reasonably possible**. There is no defined
    maximum. Consult the internship assistants before beginning a substantially
    longer internship.

## Internship Type

??? question q-tur "Can I change my internship type later?"
    No. The type is determined **before the internship starts**, and you cannot
    change the workplace, department, or type during the same internship. If
    you are unsure, consult the internship assistants first; final approval
    belongs to the **Internship advisor**.

??? question q-tur "What qualifies as a Production Internship?"
    A Production Internship must involve applying **tools and concepts from IE
    courses** in a production or manufacturing context. **Office roles connected
    to production also count**, including supply chain, production planning,
    ERP, and quality. For borderline cases, consult the
    [internship assistants](iletisim.md) and then the Internship advisor
    before the internship begins. See the [Rules](kurallar.md) and
    [Topic Guide](kurallar.md#konu-rehberi).

??? question q-tur "What qualifies as a General IE Internship?"
    Non-production roles related to **industrial engineering** count as General
    IE Internships, including finance, marketing, IT, and consulting. A **service
    industry is not required.** Any of the 40 required days outside the 15-day
    production minimum may fall into this category. See the [Rules](kurallar.md).

??? question q-tur "Must my workplace supervisor be an industrial engineer?"
    An industrial engineer is **preferred but not required**; another relevant
    engineer or manager may supervise you. Consult the Internship advisor if
    you are unsure whether a Production Internship is eligible.

## Documents

??? question q-belge "Does the department issue documents beyond those on the Forms page?"
    **No.** The department does not issue additional documents beyond those on
    the [Forms & Documents](formlar.md) page. Obtain other required paperwork,
    such as a Saturday Work Letter or payment receipt, from **your company**.

??? question q-belge "Who completes each document?"
    - The **student** completes the **EK-1 form**, has the employer sign it, and
      sends it to the Internship advisor.
    - The **workplace** completes, signs, and stamps the **Trainee Evaluation Form**.
    - The **student** completes the **internship timetable** and **Student
      Internship Survey**.
    - The **company** provides the signed or stamped **Saturday Work Letter**
      and payment receipt.

??? question q-belge "Where can I obtain the Compulsory Internship Letter?"
    Download the Turkish or English version from the
    [Forms & Documents](formlar.md) page.

??? question q-belge "When and to whom should I send the EK-1 form?"
    The EK-1 is used for insurance registration. Email it to
    [refik.gullu@bogazici.edu.tr](mailto:refik.gullu@bogazici.edu.tr) as a
    **PDF smaller than 1 MB**, no earlier than **one week** and no later than
    **two days** before the internship starts. The same form covers the
    government contribution if requested by the company. See
    [Before the Internship](surec/index.md#oncesi).

??? question q-belge "How is the insurance process completed?"
    Complete the **EK-1 form**, have the employer sign it, and email it to the
    Internship advisor. After departmental approval, the Department of
    Accounting Affairs initiates the insurance premium registration and sends
    you an **insurance registration confirmation**. You cannot start the
    internship before registration is complete. See
    [Before the Internship](surec/index.md#oncesi).

??? question q-belge "Are the EK-1 and the insurance form the same document?"
    **Yes. Insurance registration is completed through the EK-1; there is no
    separate insurance form.**

??? question q-belge "Can an internship be unpaid? Does the University still provide insurance?"
    Payment is not required, so an internship may be **unpaid**. By law,
    **Boğaziçi University still provides the insurance**. The government
    contribution applies only if requested by the company. See
    [Before the Internship](surec/index.md#oncesi).

??? question q-belge "Can I change my internship dates after sending the EK-1?"
    No. Work dates cannot be changed except in the event of a **serious health
    issue**.

??? question q-belge "My insurance was opened through the EK-1 — what do I do once the internship ends?"
    Get a **signed/stamped document** from the company confirming you completed
    the internship on the declared dates, and send it to the **Department of
    Accounting Affairs** — **no specific format is required**; your internship
    start/end dates and identifying information are enough. **Until this
    document reaches the Department of Accounting Affairs, a new insurance
    registration (a new EK-1) cannot be opened for your next internship** — in
    other words, you won't be able to start your next internship without
    sending it. Details: [After the Internship](surec/index.md#sonrasi).

??? question q-belge "In which language should I write the report, and how long may it be?"
    Write the report in **English**. Sections 1–8, which form the main report,
    may be no longer than **20 pages excluding appendices**.

??? question q-belge "Where is the timetable template?"
    It is on the [Forms & Documents](formlar.md) page under “Internship
    Timetable Template.” Use one row for each workday and four columns: Day,
    Work Accomplished, Page Nr., and Working Hours.

??? question q-belge "What is the Trainee Evaluation Form, and how do I submit it?"
    It is the official internship evaluation completed by your workplace. The
    blank form is available on the [Forms & Documents](formlar.md) page. It must
    be **both signed and stamped**; it will not be accepted if either is missing.
    Submit it with the report. It is a required document, so submission remains
    incomplete and the late penalty continues until the form is provided. See
    [After the Internship](surec/index.md#sonrasi).

??? question q-belge "May I submit documents signed with an electronic signature?"
    **Yes.** Documents signed with a valid electronic signature are accepted.

## Submission & Results

??? question q-teslim "When do I submit the documents?"
    Submit them **online within 21 days of the start of the following semester**,
    which is three weeks after classes begin. The current deadline is shown on
    the [Submission](teslim.md) page.

??? question q-teslim "What happens if I submit late?"
    **One day is deducted from your total internship days for each late day.**
    A submission with missing documents is not considered complete.

??? question q-teslim "How can I confirm that my report and documents were uploaded correctly?"
    A **success confirmation** appears when the upload is complete. If you still
    need confirmation, email the internship assistants and ask whether all
    documents were received. They will contact you if anything is missing. See
    [Submission](teslim.md) for upload instructions.

??? question q-teslim "Where can I find my internship result?"
    Results are **not published directly on this site**. See the
    [Results](sonuclar.md) page for access instructions.

??? question q-teslim "When are results announced?"
    There is no fixed schedule; results are announced **periodically**. If you
    have an urgent need related to graduation, inform the internship assistants
    **in writing**.
