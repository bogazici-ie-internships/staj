# Rules

The rules below summarize the official
**[Internship Handbook](belgeler/Revised-Handbook-of-Internship.pdf)**. The
Handbook is the binding source. Consult it and departmental announcements if you
are uncertain.

## General Information

- All Industrial Engineering (IE) students must complete **at least 40 workdays** of internship during
  the four-year undergraduate program. The 40-day internship requirement is a
  **condition for graduation**.
- **Double major (ÇAP) students:** if your primary major **is IE**, the same
  requirements apply (40 workdays, at least 15 in production). If your primary
  major **is not IE**, only **at least 15 workdays of Production Internship**
  are required on the IE side — the 40-day total requirement does not apply.
- **Minor (Yandal) students** have **no internship requirement** on the IE side.
- At least **15 of the 40 days must be in production**. This is the **only
  type requirement**: the remaining days carry no type restriction — they may be
  completed as a General IE Internship, or as further Production Internship days
  beyond the 15-day minimum. Finance, marketing, IT, and similar roles are
  accepted as General IE Internships.
- The **internship type is determined before the internship starts**, and the
  report is written accordingly. You may consult the internship assistants if
  you are unsure, but final approval belongs to the **Internship advisor**.
- Submit a signed and stamped **Trainee Evaluation Form** and the **Student
  Internship Survey** with the internship report.
- **Insurance:** By law, Boğaziçi University insures all interns against
  occupational accidents and illnesses during their internships. The required
  steps are explained under [Before the Internship](surec/index.md#oncesi).

## Internship Rules

- Internships must be completed during **summer or semester breaks**, when you
  are **not taking courses**. An internship is not accepted on dates when you
  are taking courses in the Fall semester, the Spring semester, or **Summer
  School**. You may complete an internship during Summer School if you are not
  enrolled in courses.
- Students are generally advised to begin internships after completing their
  **fourth-semester** courses. Irregular students may also begin after the third
  semester.
- Each internship must last at least **15 workdays**. The workplace, department,
  and internship type cannot change during that period.
- A **Production Internship** must involve applying concepts from IE courses in
  a production or manufacturing context. **Office roles connected to production
  also count as production.** Examples include supply chain, production
  planning, ERP, and quality. Obtain approval from the **Internship advisor**
  for borderline cases. See the [Topic Guide](#konu-rehberi) for details.
- Work completed in a university setting does not count as an internship.
- **Transfer students:** Workdays from previous internships may count toward the
  General IE Internship.
- Internships may be completed **abroad**. Most rules still apply, but
  Türkiye-specific procedures such as the **EK-1, government contribution, and
  insurance** do not. Consult the [Internship advisor or
  assistants](iletisim.md) first.

## Report and Submission Rules

- Prepare the report according to the **[Outline for Internship
  Report](rapor.md)**. You may direct questions to the internship assistants
  before submission.
- The report must be written in **English**. Tables and figures may be included;
  company information provided in Turkish does not have to be translated.
- The report may be no longer than **20 pages, excluding appendices**.
- Submit the report **within 21 days of the start of the following semester**.
- A **specific deadline is announced for each term** and shown on the
  [Submission](teslim.md) page. It may differ from the general 21-day rule; the
  announced deadline is binding.
- If you complete an internship **during a Boğaziçi academic term**, for example
  while on Erasmus or another exchange program, submit the report **within
  three weeks of completing the internship**. Late-submission rules apply
  otherwise.
- Submit the **Trainee Evaluation Form** and **Student Internship Survey** with
  the report. See [After the Internship](surec/index.md#sonrasi) for details.

!!! danger "Late-submission penalty"
    **One workday is deducted from your total internship days for each day** the
    report or any other required document is late. Submission is not considered
    complete until all missing documents are provided.

## Topic Guide {: #konu-rehberi }

The main body of your report should address topics relevant to your internship
type. You do **not** have to cover every item in the list below; select a few
relevant topics and examine them in depth.

=== "Production Internship"

    A Production Internship is completed in a **production or manufacturing
    context**; **office roles connected to production also count.** Even if you
    are not directly involved in manufacturing, you are expected to apply
    concepts from IE courses. An IE supervisor is **preferred but not
    required.** You should have worked on **at least two or three** of these areas:

    - Process analysis
    - Production planning and scheduling
    - Production or project management
    - Capacity measurement and efficiency analysis
    - Facility layout, material handling, and warehousing
    - Demand forecasting
    - Inventory management
    - Resource allocation and detailed scheduling
    - Inbound and outbound logistics planning
    - Production technologies such as JIT, CIM, FMS, GT, and CAD/CAM
    - Ergonomics and human factors
    - Work, method, and time studies
    - Lean production and Six Sigma
    - Quality planning and control, including statistical quality control
    - Production-oriented ERP modules; **data entry alone is not accepted**

=== "General IE Internship"

    A General IE Internship may be completed in any non-production role related
    to industrial engineering; a **service industry is not required.** Common
    fields include information technology, healthcare, finance, transportation,
    communications, consulting, tourism management, marketing, investment
    planning, operations planning, project management, human resources, human
    factors, and occupational health and safety.

    - Apply the IE tools and techniques listed above in a service or management
      context.
    - A General IE Internship may also be completed in a **public institution or
      NGO**. Examples include the Turkish Red Crescent, KALDER, and the
      Educational Volunteers Foundation of Turkey. Students wishing to intern at
      an NGO must obtain **verbal permission** from the Internship advisor.
    - Production Internship days beyond the requirement may also count toward
      the General IE Internship.
