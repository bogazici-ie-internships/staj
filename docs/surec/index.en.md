# Internship Process

The internship process has three stages: preparation **before the internship**,
work **during the internship**, and submission **after the internship**. Each
stage is explained below in order.

<div class="staj-flow" markdown>

## Before the Internship {: #oncesi }

Complete all of the steps below **before your internship starts**. Otherwise,
your insurance will not begin and you cannot start your internship.

These steps begin **after you find an internship position yourself**; the
department does not place students directly. Register first to receive internship
postings and announcements:

!!! tip "Register first"
    Complete this form to join the internship database and receive announcements:
    **[:material-form-select:{ .staj-fileicon } Internship Registration Form]({{ baglantilar.kayit_formu }})**

### 1. Choose an Eligible Internship

Before applying, make sure the internship meets the requirements for acceptance:

- Each internship must last **at least 15 workdays**.
- You must complete **40 workdays** in total to graduate, including at least
  **15 days of Production Internship**. The remaining 25 days may be completed
  as a **General IE Internship**.
- The internship **type is determined before the internship starts**, and the
  report is written accordingly. If you are unsure, consult the internship
  assistants; final approval belongs to the **internship supervisor**.
- You may not change the **workplace, department, or internship type** during
  the same internship, so choosing correctly from the outset is important.

!!! warning "Internships cannot overlap with courses you are taking"
    This applies to a Fall semester, a Spring semester and **Summer School**
    alike: what matters is whether you are enrolled in courses, not which term
    it is. Internships are completed during summer or semester breaks, when you
    are not taking courses.

### 2. Compulsory Internship Letter

If requested by the company, you may need to provide a **Compulsory Internship
Letter**. Turkish and English versions are available:

- :material-file-pdf-box:{ .staj-fileicon } [Compulsory Internship Letter — Turkish](../belgeler/Compulsory-Internship-Letter-TR.pdf)
- :material-file-pdf-box:{ .staj-fileicon } [Compulsory Internship Letter — English](../belgeler/Compulsory-Internship-Letter-EN.pdf)

### 3. Insurance and Government Contribution (EK-1)

By law, Boğaziçi University provides all interns with occupational accident and
occupational illness insurance during the internship. Your **insurance
registration is completed through the EK-1 form**. If the company applies for a
government contribution toward your internship pay, the same form covers that
process as well.

- :material-file-word-box:{ .staj-fileicon } [EK-1 Form](../belgeler/EK-1-Form.docx)
- :material-file-pdf-box:{ .staj-fileicon } [Government Contribution Guide](../belgeler/State-Contribution-Guide.pdf)

1. Download and complete the **EK-1 form**, then have it **signed by the employer**.
2. Email the completed form to the **internship supervisor**:
   **[Prof. Dr. Refik Güllü — refik.gullu@bogazici.edu.tr](mailto:refik.gullu@bogazici.edu.tr)**
3. The department approves the form and sends it to the **Department of
   Accounting Affairs**, which initiates your insurance premium registration.
4. The Department of Accounting Affairs emails you an **insurance registration
   confirmation**. You may begin the internship only after receiving this confirmation.

!!! danger "EK-1 submission window"
    Email the fully completed form no earlier than **one week** and no later than
    **two days** before the internship start date.

    - The EK-1 document must be a **PDF smaller than 1 MB**.
    - Once the EK-1 has been submitted, work dates cannot be changed except in
      the event of a **serious health issue**.

!!! note "If the company provides its own insurance"
    If the company provides internship insurance, the EK-1 may not be required
    and this step can be skipped. We nevertheless recommend completing it.

## During the Internship {: #sirasinda }

The recommendations below will make both your internship and the subsequent
**reporting process** easier. For binding requirements, see the
**[Rules](../kurallar.md)**.

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

-   :material-calendar-check:{ .lg .middle } **Update the timetable daily**

    ---

    Completing the required **internship timetable** each day is much easier
    than reconstructing it later.

    - One row for each **workday**, Monday through Saturday
    - Four columns: **Day · Work Accomplished · Page Nr. · Working Hours**
    - The **date range** at the top of each weekly page
    - “Page Nr.” refers to the page in **Section 6** where that work is discussed

    [:material-file-word-box:{ .staj-fileicon } Timetable template](../belgeler/Internship-Timetable.docx) · [Report Preparation](../rapor.md)

-   :material-notebook-edit:{ .lg .middle } **Collect material as you work**

    ---

    Section 6, the main body of the report, presents your analyses from an
    **industrial engineering perspective**. Process notes, data, observations,
    and the **IE tools** you use during the internship will be invaluable later.

    [Expected topics → Topic Guide](../kurallar.md#konu-rehberi)

-   :material-calendar-weekend:{ .lg .middle } **Document Saturday work**

    ---

    If you work on a Saturday, obtain a **signed or stamped letter** from the
    company so that the day can be counted. You will submit this letter after
    the internship.

    [Submission steps ↓](#sonrasi)

-   :material-chat-question:{ .lg .middle } **Ask when unsure**

    ---

    If you are unsure about the internship type, workdays, or report scope,
    contact the internship assistants **early** — these are far easier to sort
    out before the internship than after.

    [Contact](../iletisim.md)

</div>

## After the Internship {: #sonrasi }

After the internship, you submit documents separately to the **Department of
Accounting Affairs** and the **Internship Assistants**.

### A. Documents for the Department of Accounting Affairs

Obtain a **company-approved document confirming that you completed the
internship within the dates previously reported to the Department of Accounting
Affairs**. Request this document from the company and send it directly to the
Department of Accounting Affairs.

- This is **not** the Trainee Evaluation Form.
- A scanned copy may be sent by email.

!!! info "If the University insured you through the EK-1"
    This document closes the insurance process initiated through the **EK-1**
    before the internship. It confirms that you completed the internship on the
    declared dates. This step may not apply if the company provided your insurance.

### B. Documents for the Internship Assistants

!!! danger "Submission deadline"
    Submit these documents **online within 21 days of the start of the following
    semester**, which is three weeks after classes begin. See the
    [Rules](../kurallar.md) for late-submission penalties.

    For submission instructions, see **[Submission](../teslim.md)**.

#### Required documents

1. **Signed and stamped Trainee Evaluation Form**
   No envelope is required because the form is submitted online.
   → Blank form: [Trainee Evaluation Form](../belgeler/Trainee-Evaluation-Form.pdf)

2. **Student Internship Survey**
   → Blank form: [Student Internship Survey](../belgeler/Student-Internship-Survey.pdf)

3. **Internship report prepared according to the rules**
   See the [Internship Handbook](../belgeler/Revised-Handbook-of-Internship.pdf)
   and [Report Preparation](../rapor.md).

#### Optional documents

1. **Payment receipt** — required if the company paid the intern and is applying
   for reimbursement of part of that payment.

2. **Saturday Work Letter** — a signed or stamped company letter confirming
   weekend work.

!!! note "When submission is considered complete"
    During submission, you confirm that you submitted the report and all
    documents that day and provide your email address. A submission with missing
    documents **is not complete**, and time continues to run until the missing
    documents arrive. Once you supply them, the submission is complete.
